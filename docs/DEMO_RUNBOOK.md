# Demo runbook — what exists today

The Build Bible's five-minute runbook (§44) assumes all three surfaces. Only **Lens + the
agent** are built, so this is that runbook cut down to what you can actually put on a
screen without lying. It runs about **2 minutes 45**, which leaves room for the parts you
add as Sanctuary lands.

**Every live step has a pre-recorded twin one keypress away: `Alt+Shift+D`.**

---

## Before you walk on

```
[ ] Extension loaded unpacked, no console errors on your 5 test sites
[ ] Edge deployed AND reachable — check it in the extension's Settings → Test the connection
[ ] SETU_ALLOWED_EXTENSION_IDS contains your extension id (chrome://extensions)
[ ] Cache pre-warmed: run every demo input once, the night before
[ ] Demo mode verified WITH THE NETWORK DISABLED (Alt+Shift+D, then run every beat)
[ ] Backup video open in a background tab, paused at 0:00 — not in a folder, open
[ ] Bookmarks bar cleared, one window, no personal tabs, notifications OFF
[ ] Zoom 110–125% so the back row can read the load score
[ ] Laptop on mains, sleep disabled, Do Not Disturb ON
[ ] Screen recording software OFF — it steals frames
[ ] Mobile hotspot charged and tested. Never demo on venue wifi.
```

---

## The beats

### 0:00–0:30 · The wall

Open the university portal. Say the problem before you show anything:

> "One in five people open a browser and hit a wall. Not a wall of information — a wall of
> *load*."

### 0:30–1:00 · The measurement

Point at the badge on the SETU icon. **Point with your hand** — physical gesture puts the
room's eyes where you want them.

> "That's 84. That's not our opinion, that's a measurement — structural complexity, text
> density, motion, decisions above the fold, interruptions. Computed on this laptop, in
> forty milliseconds, with no network call."

Open the panel → **This page** tab. The breakdown is there in words: *visual clutter, too
many choices at once, popups and sticky bars.*

### 1:00–1:30 · Breathe

Move the cursor erratically and rage-click for about eight seconds.

**If it does not fire within ten seconds, do not wait.** Panel → **This page** → *Demo
controls* → **Trigger the pause offer now**. Nobody in the room can tell the difference,
and Risk #3 in the register exists precisely so this is not a gamble.

The page dims. One breath cycle — in, hold, out. *Then* the offer appears.

Say the line that wins the disability-inclusion judge:

> "Read what it says. *'This page looks intense.'* SETU never comments on the person. It
> comments on the page. That distinction is the whole ethics of this feature."

Press **Yes, simplify**. **84 → 19.**

> "A 77% reduction in measured cognitive load."

### 1:30–2:00 · Wifi off

**Turn the wifi off.** Visibly. On the menu bar, where they can see it.

Toggle Focus Mode off and on. Toggle bionic reading. Move the line guide. The load score
recomputes. Everything keeps working.

> "Everything you have just seen ran on this CPU. Zero servers. Zero cost. Zero data
> leaving. Our cost curve tracks intelligence used, not users served."

**This is the highest-ROI thirty seconds in the entire pitch and it costs zero build
hours.** Rehearse it until it is boring.

Turn the wifi back on.

### 2:00–2:30 · START

Panel → **Start**. Type: `apply for my exam re-evaluation`.

The consent dialog appears first. Do not skip past it — it is the point:

> "Before anything is sent, I see exactly what is sent. Two personal details were removed
> first."

Accept. Steps appear with minutes, effort, and how each *step* feels — never how the
person feels.

> "The gap was never knowledge. It was the first action."

### 2:30–2:45 · Trust, then silence

Panel → **Trust**.

> "Every action SETU has taken. Which ran on this device, which left it, how many bytes,
> how many personal details were removed first. Most products put privacy in a policy. We
> put it in a log you can read."

Then, without explanation, press **`Alt+Shift+Q`**.

Everything collapses to one sentence and one button.

**Say nothing.** Three full seconds. Let it land.

## Drive one segment by keyboard only

Do the Focus Mode beat with `Alt+Shift+F`, and mention it in passing:

> "And yes — the whole thing is keyboard-operable, because it has to be."

An accessibility product demoed entirely by mouse is a contradiction judges notice.

---

## When it breaks

Memorise this verbatim:

> **"That's the live network being the live network — here's the same flow from thirty
> minutes ago."**

Then switch. Calm recovery reads as competence; panic reads as fragility. **Rehearse a
deliberate failure at least once** so the recovery is muscle memory rather than improvisation.

---

## Do not demo

- **COMMANDER on a site you do not control.** It works, and it is genuinely well-guarded,
  but a live agent on someone else's DOM is the single highest-variance thing you could put
  on a stage. Show the *plan* and the confirmation gate on your own page, and say plainly:
  "today it acts on the current page, with confirmation — cross-site autonomy is roadmap."
- **Anything requiring Gemini Nano (L1).** Feature-detect it beforehand. If it happens to
  work on your machine, show it with the wifi off as a bonus — never as a required beat.
- **Sanctuary or the mobile app.** They do not exist yet. Say what is built and what is
  next; scope discipline reads as maturity.
